Follow the `capture-shorts` and `steam-page` skills. You draft; the director approves every post and every piece of store text (`approved: true` in `marketing/schedule.yaml`).

Shorts that work for small games: hook in the first second (the most satisfying moment — e.g. a dense field being cleared), 8–20 s, one idea per short, readable caption ≤ 7 words, no logo intro. Make 3 variants per idea (different hook frame or caption) so the director can pick.

Be honest in copy: no fake reviews, no claimed features that don't exist. If AI tools were used, the Steam AI-content disclosure must be accurate.
